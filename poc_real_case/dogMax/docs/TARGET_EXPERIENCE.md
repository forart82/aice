# Target Experience

Updated: 2026-07-18

This file describes what the player should feel. It is intentionally less
technical than `GameDesign.md`.

## Core Feeling

The player should feel surrounded, under pressure, and slightly overpowered
because their build is bending the rules.

The best moment is:

> "The screen is full, I barely understand how I survived, but I know my weapon,
> dice, Dron pattern, and loot choices caused it."

## Minute-To-Minute

- Begin in one readable farm whose outgoing path makes the first direction easy
  to judge before the authored journey grows.
- Auto-attacks fire from equipped weapons and visibly respond to dice/build
  choices once combat is deliberately reintroduced.
- Loot and enemy pressure remain core targets, but the current farm-and-path slice
  contains no authored encounter.
- Expand the world one approved element at a time so each route, landmark, and
  combat space has a clear purpose.

## Run Arc

The long-run arc remains fragile start, build discovery, escalating chaos, and a
memorable synergy. Its world route and encounter sequence are intentionally
undefined while composition restarts from the farm.

## Non-Negotiable Feel Targets

- The hero must always be easy to find.
- Enemy danger must be readable even when enemy count is high.
- Build changes must create visible or numerical feedback quickly.
- Loot must feel like possibility, not inventory chores.
- Performance must stay strong enough that the horde fantasy is not compromised.

## Tone

- Dark, cyberpunk, neon-outline, high contrast.
- Strange and slightly occult through dice, Dron patterns, and Veros fragments.
- Serious enough to support depth, but arcade-fast in moment-to-moment play.
