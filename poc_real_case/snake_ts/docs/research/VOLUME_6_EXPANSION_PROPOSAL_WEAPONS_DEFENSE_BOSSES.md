## 5. The 4 New Active Game Mechanics

### 5.1 Tactical Autotomy (Emergency Tail Jettison)
- **Execution:** Press `[Space]` or double-tap `Dash` while low on HP.
- **Effect:** Jettisons rear segments $k \dots N-1$. Jettisoned wagons detonate in an $80\text{ AoE}$ kinetic shockwave, clearing mobs and granting $+1.0\text{s}$ i-frames and $+45\%$ turning agility ($\omega_{\text{turn}}$).

### 5.2 Centripetal Tail-Whip Collisions ($v = \omega \cdot r$)
- **Execution:** Perform a sharp 180° whip turn while at high speed.
- **Effect:** Trailing caudal wagons accelerate to $v_{\text{tail}} = v_{\text{head}} + \omega \cdot r$ (up to $1600\text{ px/s}$), dealing crushing blunt impact damage and knocking back surrounding mobs.

### 5.3 Closed-Loop Ouroboros Constriction
- **Execution:** Slither in a circle so the head connects with a trailing segment.
- **Effect:** Forms a topological stasis polygon ($W(P) \ne 0$). Encircled enemies are immobilized and crushed ($\text{DPS} \propto \frac{\text{Area}_0}{A(t)}$), while all trapped food compresses into an **Astral Prism** (+300% XP).

### 5.4 The 28-Synergy Alchemical Fusion Engine
- **Execution:** Install complementary elemental modules on adjacent wagons.
- **Effect:** Unlocks named super-weapons (*Superconductor Surge, Obsidian Carapace, Miasma Combustion, Prismatic Aegis, Temporal Singularity, Railgun Flak*).
