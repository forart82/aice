## 1. Feature Overview & Requirements

- **High Concept:** Execute a massive, multi-iteration campaign simulation campaign under strict Rule 11 (HEBAR / Anti-Cheat Standard) across 100+ full playthroughs for all 5 serpent classes (Emerald, Stalker, Hydra, Storm, Solar) on Floors 1–25.
- **Target Systems:**
  - `src/sim/ai/AutonomousBotPlayer.ts` & `src/sim/ai/BotTelemetryLogger.ts`
  - `src/ui/screens/MenuScreen.ts` & `src/ui/screens/ClassSelectScreen.ts`
  - `src/sim/GameEngine.ts`, `src/sim/SnakePlayer.ts`, `src/sim/CombatSystem.ts`
  - `src/tests/MassAutonomousCampaignTelemetry.test.ts`
- **Core HEBAR Compliance Mandates (Rule 11):**
  1. **Menu & Modal Lifecycle:** All runs initialize from `GameState.Menu`, simulate UI clicks on `MenuScreen` (mode selection) and `ClassSelectScreen` (class card click, helmet vault equip, start run button), and transition into `GameState.Playing` legitimately.
  2. **Physical Input Compliance:** All steering, dashing, and active skills flow strictly through `IInput` controller contracts (`SetDirection`, `RequestDash()`, `RequestSkill()`). No out-of-band coordinate or velocity teleportation.
  3. **Legitimate Draft Selection:** Card upgrades during `GameState.LevelUp` pick strictly from active `CurrentDraftCards` generated by RNG. No artificial perk mutations.
  4. **Cranial Lab Economic Gate:** In-run module/augment purchases validate challenge unlock gates via `MetaProgression.isTierUnlocked()` and deduct cost atomically via `SnakePlayer.spendOuro()`.
  5. **Head-Centric Serpentine Vault:** Helmets equip strictly from owned `MetaProgression.Helmets` vault drops. All modules and augments reside strictly on the Serpent Head.
