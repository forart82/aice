## 1. Feature Overview & Requirements

- **High Concept:** Achieve 100% visual parity for all 6 enemies, 8 boss archetypes, attack telegraphs, and cosmic nebula backdrops between Canvas2D and WebGL rendering engines.
- **Target Systems:**
  - `src/render/webgl/WebGLGameRenderer.ts`
  - `src/render/webgl/WebGLAssetAtlas.ts`
  - `src/render/EnemyRenderer.ts`
  - `src/render/BossRenderer.ts`
  - `src/render/TelegraphRenderer.ts`
- **Mechanics & Visual Reference:** [`ORACLE.md#section-4`](file:///home/forart/dev/snake_ts/ORACLE.md)

### Detailed Requirements:
1. **Cosmic Nebula Backdrop**:
   - `WebGLAssetAtlas.getBackdropTexture()` must always upload and bind the exact canvas generated by `ArenaBackdrop.bakeBackdrop()` (which contains the 5-stop linear gradient along the -32° galactic spine, elliptical core bulge, and cosmic vignette).
   - In `WebGLGameRenderer.drawBackdrop()`, if the fallback branch executes (e.g. mock WebGL without textures), it must NOT draw a single flat solid rectangle with `GLPrimitive.Quad`. It must render a 16-band gradient matching the 5-stop linear gradient, concentric focal spheres for the core bulge, and perimeter vignette.
   - `WebGLGameRenderer.getBackdropTexture()` delegates to `WebGLAssetAtlas.getBackdropTexture()` ensuring unified caching.
2. **6 Enemies Parity (Spitter, Drone, Phantom, Bruiser, Swarmling, Scarab)**:
   - Verify hull shapes, eye glints, and status indicators (freeze/slow cyan ring, burn orange ring, HP bar) between `EnemyRenderer.ts` and `WebGLGameRenderer.ts`.
   - Support `bruiser` alias alongside `brute` and `dasher` in both renderers.
3. **8 Bosses Parity (Centipede, Colossus, Frostwyrm, Chronowyrm, Gorgon, Leviathan, Ouroboros Prime, Void)**:
   - Ensure all 8 boss archetypes have custom head geometries implemented in both `BossRenderer.ts` (Canvas2D) and `WebGLGameRenderer.ts` (WebGL).
   - Support `void` alias alongside `void_devourer` in both renderers.
   - Serpentine continuous muscular spine foundation ribbons connecting head to segments in both Canvas2D and WebGL.
   - Expanding attack telegraphs (circle, cone, line/corridor, ring/warp, hazard) render progress-expanding fills ($r \times p$) and warning boundaries in both Canvas2D and WebGL.

## 2. Test Plan & Assertions (Red Phase)

- **Test Suite Location:** `src/tests/EnemiesBossesAndCosmicNebulaVisualParity.test.ts`
- **Assertions:**
  1. `WebGLAssetAtlas.getBackdropTexture` binds and uploads textures from `ArenaBackdrop.bakeBackdrop()` and caches by `(w, h, floor)`.
  2. `WebGLGameRenderer.drawBackdrop` fallback branch draws a multi-strip gradient across the galactic spine instead of a single flat solid `GLPrimitive.Quad`.
  3. All 6 enemies (`spitter`, `drone`, `phantom`, `brute`/`bruiser`, `swarmling`, `scarab`) render in both `EnemyRenderer` and `WebGLGameRenderer` with status rings and health bars.
  4. All 8 bosses (`centipede`, `colossus`, `frostwyrm`, `chronowyrm`, `gorgon`, `leviathan`, `ouroboros_prime`, `void`) render with custom head geometries and spine ribbons in both `BossRenderer` and `WebGLGameRenderer`.
  5. Expanding attack telegraphs (`circle`, `cone`, `line`, `ring`, `hazard`) render in both `TelegraphRenderer` / `BossRenderer` and `WebGLGameRenderer` with progress-scaled geometry.
