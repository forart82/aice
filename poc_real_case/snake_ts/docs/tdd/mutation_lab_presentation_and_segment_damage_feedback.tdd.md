## 1. Feature Overview & Requirements

- **High Concept:**
  1. **Mutation Lab Presentation & Massive Serpentine Scaling:**
     - **Adaptive Multi-Row Serpentine Poses:** Compute boustrophedon multi-row S-curves in `computeSerpentinePoses`, preventing node clustering/overlap for 10, 20, 50+ body segments.
     - **Canvas Node Identity Labels:** Render high-contrast badges for "👑 Head" and each body segment `#1`, `#2`, ..., `#N` directly on/above each node.
     - **Real-Time HP Text & Mini Health Bars:** Render individual part HP status (`25/25`, `⚠️ 12/25`) and colored mini health bars under each node so damage and health state are immediately evident.
     - **Interactive Canvas Drag-Panning & Wheel Zoom:** Enable smooth camera panning (`mousedown` + drag) and mouse wheel zooming (`wheel` event) clamped between 0.4x and 4.0x.
     - **Segment Group / Pagination Selector:** Provide `[All]`, `[1-10]`, `[11-20]`, `[21-30]`, ... page filters in the drawer that auto-synchronize with part selection, ensuring fluid navigation even with 50+ segments.
     - **Pure Inspection & Organ Customization:** Remove manual segment growth (`+ Grow`) from the Mutation Lab, dedicating segment growth purely to in-game food ingestion while the Lab focuses on organ installation, hardpoints, and cranial augments.
  2. **In-Game Body Part & Head Damage Visual Feedback:**
     - **Damaged Body Segments:** Pulsing damage warning glow, cracked carapace fracture lines (`hpPct < 0.5`), mini health arcs (`hpPct * Tau`), and localized hit flash (`hitFlash > 0`).
     - **Cranial Head Damage:** When `headHp < headMaxHp`, render cranial health arc, pulsing warning halos, and skull fracture lines (`headHpPct < 0.6`).
     - **Localized Damage FX:** Particle bursts and floating damage numbers (`-X`) rendered directly at the exact damaged segment or head coordinates during enemy bites, projectile impacts, and self-collision damage.
     - **Enhanced HUD Vitals:** HUD health display explicitly highlights the Head and Body parts breakdown (`👑 Head: X/Y • 🟢 Body: A/B`).

- **Target Systems:**
  - `src/ui/screens/SnakeLabScreen.ts` (Canvas node labels, real-time HP badges, interactive drag-pan & wheel zoom, segment group pagination)
  - `src/render/SnakeRenderer.ts` (Segment & head damage visualization, cranial health arcs, skull fracture lines, cracked carapace, warning halos)
  - `src/render/GameRenderer.ts` (Pass `headHp`, `headMaxHp`, `hp`, `maxHp`, `hitFlash`, and socket data to `ISnakeRenderData`)
  - `src/sim/SnakePlayer.ts` (`HitFlash`, localized damage burst & text at segment/head coordinates)
  - `src/ui/HudView.ts` (Headless-safe HUD vitals with Head & Body HP breakdown)

## 2. Test Plan & Assertions (Red Phase)

- **Test Suite Location:** `src/tests/MutationLabAndDamageFeedback.test.ts`
- **Assertions:**
  1. **Adaptive Serpentine Layout Non-Overlap**: For segment counts of 3, 8, 15, 25, and 50, all node poses generated by `computeSerpentinePoses` have positive spacing between consecutive nodes (min 10px distance), distinct coordinates, and remain within canvas boundaries.
  2. **Interactive Canvas Drag-Panning & Wheel Zooming**: `SnakeLabScreen.panCamera(dx, dy)` updates camera target coordinates; `SnakeLabScreen.zoomCamera(factor)` adjusts zoom clamped in `[0.4, 4.0]`.
  3. **Segment Group Pagination & Auto-Synchronization**: `SnakeLabScreen.computeSegmentGroups` generates correct ranges for 10 and 50 segments (`['All', '1-10', '11-20', ...]`); selecting segment index 24 auto-selects group page 2 (`21-30`).
  4. **Segment Navigation Controls**: Smooth prev/next cycling between Overview (-2), Head (-1), and segments 0..N-1.
  5. **Segment Damage Localization & Hit Flash**: Taking damage or damaging a segment triggers localized `hitFlash > 0` and reduces HP.
  6. **Self-Collision Damage**: Non-adjacent segment intersections apply self-damage and localized hitFlash.
  7. **SnakeRenderer Data & Cranial/Segment Health**: `ISnakeRenderData` receives `headHp`, `headMaxHp`, `segments[i].hp`, `maxHp`, and `hitFlash`.
  8. **HUD Vitals Head/Body Breakdown**: `HudView.refresh` populates `hud-hp-val` with `👑 Head: X/Y • 🟢 Body: A/B`.
